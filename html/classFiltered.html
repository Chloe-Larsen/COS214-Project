Detailed Description

Iterator decorator that filters items from an inner iterator.

Wraps another Iterator and only yields WorkComponents that satisfy the supplied FilterCriteria. Non-matching components are skipped transparently

Note
Filtered takes ownership of the inner iterator and deletes it in its destructor. It does NOT take ownership of the FilterCriteria
